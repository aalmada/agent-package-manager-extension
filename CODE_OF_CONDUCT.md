# Code of conduct

## Expectations

Everyone participating in this project's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other community spaces must:

- Be respectful and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Discuss ideas and code constructively, not the worth or identity of the person contributing.
- Accept feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and give others room to learn.
- Respect privacy; do not publish personal information, credentials, or private communications without permission.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Ask the repository maintainer, [@aalmada](https://github.com/aalmada), for moderation help in the affected thread. If reporting publicly would expose sensitive information, open an issue asking for a private contact channel without naming other participants or including incident details. The maintainer will arrange follow-up.

For security vulnerabilities, use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, or restrict participation. Depending on the severity and repetition, moderation may include a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Reports will be handled with discretion; information will be shared only as needed to investigate and respond.
